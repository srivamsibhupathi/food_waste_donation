ABOUT FOODLINK

Technology with a human purpose.

FoodLink is a college project prototype designed to make local food rescue faster, more transparent and more accessible.

The Problem

Large amounts of edible food are discarded while communities face food insecurity. Donors often lack a simple way to connect surplus with trusted organizations.

Our Solution

FoodLink creates a local digital bridge between donors, NGOs and volunteers with matching, tracking and impact reporting.

Mission

Make food rescue as easy as ordering a meal.

Vision

A future where good food is shared before it is wasted.
